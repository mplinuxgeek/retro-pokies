# Notice: Retro Pokies

Retro Pokies is a browser emulator for the Aristocrat MK6 platform. It is released under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`). The licence covers this software only, not any ROM, game name, artwork or sound.

* **No ROMs are included or hosted.** This site contains only an emulator (JavaScript), per-game configuration maps (memory addresses and key numbers) and a title catalogue. Each player supplies their own ROM zip files from a machine they own or are entitled to use. ROMs are stored in the player's own browser (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded.
* **Play money only.** A simulator for study and preservation. No real money can be wagered, paid out or won; credits have no cash value. Results say nothing about any real machine.
