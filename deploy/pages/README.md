# Retro Pokies

A play-money slot machine simulator that runs in your browser, for study and preservation. It emulates the Aristocrat MK6 platform in JavaScript.

**No ROMs are included.** You need your own legally obtained ROM zip files, from a machine you own or are entitled to use.

## Using it

1. Open `games.html` (the site's front page).
2. Press **Add games** and pick your zip files (several at once are fine), or drag and drop them onto the page.
3. Press a game to play.

ROMs are stored in your browser (IndexedDB) on this device and are never uploaded. **Remove from this browser** on a game's card deletes its copy.

## Requirements

A current desktop or mobile browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) with JavaScript, Web Audio and IndexedDB enabled. Once loaded it works offline as a PWA (install it from the browser menu). On iPhone and iPad use Share > Add to Home Screen, or Safari may clear saved data after 7 days.
